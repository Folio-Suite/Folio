# Traditional Write icons

Two opaque, full-bleed 1024 × 1024 PNGs for catalog experiments. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized to the exact requested dimensions. Not assigned to an application target.

Default prompt: Re-render the approved glass fountain-pen nib over a peach vaporwave sunset and indigo water, with cyan/pink refractive edges and a diffuse reflection beneath the nib. Full-bleed square, no text or rounded tile boundary.

Dark prompt: Preserve the default composition and nib geometry; shift to smoked violet glass, midnight indigo sky, muted coral sun, and subdued water reflections. Full-bleed square, no text.
